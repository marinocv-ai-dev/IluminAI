# Mentor session: venture strategy (10 min)

## Opening (~20 s)
"We're building IluminAI, an AI evidence map for rare diseases. We want to close two gaps. First, families feel alone after a diagnosis, so we connect them with other families, patient groups and research they can join. Second, researchers work in silos. Our AI suggests connections between diseases that share similar symptoms, related genes or the same biological process, and it shows the source for every suggestion. Families and researchers use it for free, and they help validate what the AI finds. Insurers, hospitals and governments are the buyers. We have three quick questions."

**Short version (~10 s):**
"IluminAI connects isolated rare disease families with each other, and helps researchers find related diseases through shared symptoms, genes or biological processes, with a source for every link. Families and researchers use it free and validate the results. Insurers, hospitals and governments pay. Three quick questions."

## 1. First buyer
"Our buyers are insurers, hospitals and governments. Insurers save on long diagnostic journeys and repeated tests. Hospitals send patients to the right specialist or trial faster. Governments get data to set rare disease priorities. Which one would you go after first, and what proof would they need before paying?"

## 2. Human in the loop
"Families and researchers don't just use the tool. They validate the AI's connections, add missing evidence and build community around each disease. Experts review everything before it becomes data. Do investors see that as a real advantage, or as a risk? And how should we pitch it?"

## 3. Planned demo and 10× claim
"Our planned demo follows a family with a rare disease that has no approved treatment. The AI lights up a 3D evidence map, explains why a therapy for a related disease could actually harm them, and ends with a trial they can join this week. We want to claim we cut the time from diagnosis to joining research by ten times. Would that be believable to judges, and what starting number should we use to prove it?"

## If there's time
"Would this be a good fit for the Venture Lab, and what should we show in twelve weeks?"
